The Origin Story
One operations leader.
Zero developers.

In April 2026, I sat down with one question: why does it take weeks to know what's happening across our 44 stores? I'm not a developer. I've never written a line of code professionally. I run operations for Tim Hortons India — that's my world.

But I had a vision: a single platform where area managers submit compliance photos, AI grades them instantly, tasks are tracked, performance is visible in real-time, and leadership gets intelligent reports — automatically, every day.

Using AI as my co-developer, I built that platform from scratch. No agency. No IT team. No outsourcing. What would have taken a development firm 12–18 months and significant cost — we built in under 90 days, running live, serving real stores, with real AI.
