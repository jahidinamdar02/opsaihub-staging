Platform Capabilities

Everything an Area Manager Needs

12 integrated modules covering every aspect of café operations — from daily tasks to long-term performance tracking.

Daily Checklists
Day-wise operational checklists (Mon–Sun) with specific parameters for each day — store visits, compliance, coverage, and more.
Performance Scores
12+ scoring parameters including sales compliance, store visits, FDU compliance, training completion, donut champions, and FOSTAC certification.
Events & Calendar
Company events, training schedules, IPL match days, and critical operational dates — all visible in one calendar view with category filters.
FDU Compliance
Daily Food Display Unit photo submissions with AI-powered analysis. History tracking by area manager and store, compliance percentages, and gap identification.
Training & People Health
Training completion tracking, tenure analysis, FOSTAC certification pipeline, Tuesday AI Learning quizzes, and people health scores by region.
HOD Dashboard
Head of Department view with heatmap of all 44 stores, region-wise compliance, and drill-down capability into individual AM performance.
Cash Handling Audit
Weekly audits for store managers, monthly for area managers. Excel-based with automated email triggers to finance team and HOD.
Settings & Profile
User profile management, photo uploads, dark mode toggle, and personal preferences — all persisted via JWT authentication.
Live Activity Feed
Real-time feed of store visits, checklist submissions, and compliance updates — Instagram-style with reactions, comments, and auto-posted content.
Store Allocation
Complete store-to-AM mapping across 42+ stores, region-wise allocation, and TBA (To Be Assigned) tracking for open positions.
Push Notifications
Web push notifications for critical updates, task reminders, and compliance deadlines — ensuring AMs never miss important actions.

Execution Timeline

Build & Rollout Plan

From initial MVP to full operational platform — the journey of OpsAIHub.

Phase 1 — April 2026
MVP Launch
Core platform with PIN-based auth, daily checklists, store visit logging, basic performance scores, and single-file server architecture (1,670 lines).
Phase 2 — May 2026
Feature Expansion
Events calendar, FDU compliance tracking, training health module, cash audit system, and Instagram-style team feed.
Phase 3 — June 2026
AI Integration & Scale
Gemini AI for donut analysis, Timsy 2.0 assistant, server modularization (4393 → 250 lines + 10 routes), JWT auth, Jest test suite (56 tests), Hallmark design system.
Phase 4 — June 18–24, 2026
7-Day Build Sprint
Timsy 2.0 enhancements → Anomaly Detection → Store Sparklines → Web Push Notifications → Task Command → Polish & Launch.
Phase 5 — July 2026
Full Rollout & Optimization
Production deployment, performance monitoring, user feedback iteration, and advanced analytics dashboard for leadership.
